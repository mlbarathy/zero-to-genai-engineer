## What is inside the interactive deck

68 stages · 30 diagrams · 87 interview reveal cards · 14 trap MCQs · 20 Play controls.

No stage is bare text: every one carries a diagram, an interactive widget, interview cards or ship links.

- Dotted chapter rail, stage dots, and a live score counter for the trap questions
- 20 Plays: inverted index, TF-IDF scoring, cosine drag, embedding ladder, attention weights, GPT scale, RLHF against DPO, BPE merges, the sampling pipeline with a live probability distribution, providers, prompt anatomy, the DSPy optimizer ladder, LCEL pipe, the `/goal` loop, RAG pipeline, RRF fusion, LangGraph retry edge, HITL pause and resume, tool against skill, AWS request path, LoRA multiply
- Full Session 00 to 07 interview bank (reveal cards) plus S09 to S15 hireable questions and a finale trap MCQ round
- Ship links: `github.com/nursnaaz/...` and Pages only (no local `../` paths)
- No em dashes

Keys: ↓ next · left rail = chapter · right dots = slide. On a phone, diagrams swipe sideways.

`OUTLINE.md` is generated from the deck, so regenerate it if you edit stages.
